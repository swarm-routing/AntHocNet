# v2.7.x image control: the published numbers on the ns3-dev campaign image

[← Benchmark index](../benchmarks.md) · [Methodology](methodology.md#reproducing-via-ci-manual-dispatch) · [v2.6.0 harness control](v2.6.0-harness-control.md)

Campaigns are moving from this repository's own simulator image to the org's
`ns3-dev` campaign image (swarm-routing/.github#107). The move changes the base
(Ubuntu 22.04, gcc-11 to Ubuntu 24.04, gcc-13), the configure line and the
module list. For the MANET families and the ISL grid it also changes ns-3
(3.42 to 3.48). For the LEO and SAGIN families ns-3 stays at 3.48, but the
profile changes from `default` to `release`. Byte-identical results across
that change are not expected. A published number may not be compared with a
post-move number until the difference is measured. This page is that
measurement ([#867](https://github.com/swarm-routing/AntHocNet/issues/867)).

## Plan and rule

- **Plan:** [`tools/bench/campaigns/v2.7.x-image-control.yaml`](../../tools/bench/campaigns/v2.7.x-image-control.yaml),
  generation `ctl867`, accepted by the maintainer on 2026-11-10 (#867).
  - **Cells:** one control cell per family that has published numbers, all
    from the v2.4.0 generation: `grid-rwp-tworay`, `static-mesh`, `fanet-main`,
    `vanet-sparse`, `disaster-main`, `sat-failcell`, `leo-walker16-*` and
    `sagin-walker16-*` (one cell per arm, as published).
    - The VANET cell is `vanet-sparse` instead of `vanet-main`, for runner time.
    - No comparator anchors.
  - **Seeds and inputs:** 20 seeds per cell, with the published cell's seed
    blocks and command line.
  - **Commit:** both arms run one AntHocNet commit, `source_sha`
    `b531d5d40c199b159527257119f872e3da7eeb5b`. Each cell checks it out
    whatever ref it was dispatched at (#905), and every collected cell's
    `##PROV##` names it.
- **Arms:**

  | arm | role | image | how it runs | dispatched by |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | `old` | control | the image the published cell's `##PROV##` names, by the digest its frozen tag resolves to: `ghcr.io/danieljoppi/ns3@sha256:af6b50155528164de33764f059a2736d31bf4cca1c4778009c93fb6620ddc95e` (`3.42-opt`, release profile: MANET families and ISL) and `ghcr.io/danieljoppi/ns3@sha256:8b5a0e308dd4bbb55b3124cfff18f24746ba598646f90f24d38a6e4bfdf06c65` (`3.48`, default profile: LEO and SAGIN) | the legacy in-job build of `source_sha` | `tools/bench/campaign.py` |
  | `dev` | test | `ghcr.io/swarm-routing/anthocnet-ns3-campaign@sha256:ee930d8c6592b012a4ec321f0f255945f66cbf505e5850ab7fea683f1e2aba68`, built by `campaign-image.yml` run 42847354947 from `source_sha` on `ghcr.io/swarm-routing/ns3-dev:3.48-optimized-fb7596b@sha256:5585a93c5cea1980592539e2d26a4e111e0ff4b0cc63b3dfd6ea5de459d80d3f` | prebuilt, `--no-build`, through swarm-routing/.github's `reusable-benchmark-cell.yml` | `ns3-campaign` (swarm-routing/ns3-tools) |

- **Read 1, old vs published** (`tools/bench/harness_control.py`): every
  `##RUN##`, `##ROUTE##` and `##COMMON##` row of the old arm must equal the
  committed v2.4.0 row for the same seed and arm, byte for byte.
  - The `danieljoppi` tags were re-pushed on 2026-11-03, after the v2.4.0
    runs, so their digests are not provably the bytes those runs pulled.
  - A PASS shows that the old arm stands in for the published numbers, and so
    that the legacy image is the right one.
- **Read 2, dev vs old** (`tools/bench/campaign.py verdicts`, which runs
  `bench_parse.py --ab --proto P` for every protocol of every cell): per-seed
  paired deltas over 20 seeds, each with its 95% CI and Wilcoxon p.
  - A `PAIRED-*` verdict other than `PAIRED-NOISE` means that at least one of
    dPDR, d_d99 and d_NRL has a CI that excludes zero
    ([`bench_parse.py`](../../tools/bench/bench_parse.py)).
  - Both arms run the same commit, so a delta is the image's effect: the
    toolchain, the ns-3 version or profile, and the configure line.

Facts from the files, beyond the script's verdicts:
- In the LEO and SAGIN cells, the dev arm's `##RUN##`/`##ROUTE##`/`##COMMON##` rows equal the old arm's byte for byte.
- In `sat-failcell`, the oracle's rows are equal. The 20 anthocnet `##RUN##` rows differ only in the second decimal of two columns (e.g. seed 1: `10.16` vs `10.15` and `1.09` vs `1.10`), and every paired delta rounds to zero.

**What this page does not decide.** Whether the non-NOISE deltas matter for
any published claim, and so whether post-move numbers may be compared with
published ones in the five MANET-type families, is the maintainer's call (#867).
The rule in [methodology](methodology.md#reproducing-via-ci-manual-dispatch)
is proposed on these verdicts and awaits that decision.
