## What it stresses

The paper's base scenario (AntHocNet's design regime). It is also the
**Broch/Perkins 1500×300 m / 50-node field**, which makes it the calibration
target of the [validation anchors](../methodology.md#validation-anchors-known-expected-results) —
AODV is known from the literature to deliver ~90–100% there at low mobility.

[`ns3/tools/run-scenarios.py`](../../../ns3/tools/run-scenarios.py)
(`scenario=paper`: 50 nodes, 1500×300 m, random-waypoint 20 m/s / 30 s pause,
20 CBR sources of one 64-byte packet/s, 300 m range, 2 Mbit/s, 802.11 DCF). The
nightly `benchmarks` workflow runs the taxonomy at 20 seeds (#977).

## Results

> **Caveat — `large-scale` and `heavy-load` currently reflect an open
> defect.** [#173](https://github.com/swarm-routing/AntHocNet/issues/173)
> (P1, open): with `enableMultipath` on, reactive forward ants are bounded
> neither by a broadcast budget nor by `(src,seq)` duplicate suppression, so
> route discovery floods combinatorially in dense graphs. Read those two rows
> as a bug report, not as protocol character. See
> [docs/fidelity.md](../../fidelity.md).
