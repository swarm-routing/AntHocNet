## What it stresses

The fast CI regime; AntHocNet's hard case. A small, dense, barely-mobile field
that runs cheaply enough for the nightly `benchmarks` workflow, so it is the
scenario that gives the quick regression signal.

## Results

> **Caveat — `large-scale` and `heavy-load` currently reflect an open
> defect.** [#173](https://github.com/swarm-routing/AntHocNet/issues/173)
> (P1, open): with `enableMultipath` on, reactive forward ants are bounded
> neither by a broadcast budget nor by `(src,seq)` duplicate suppression, so
> route discovery floods combinatorially in dense graphs. Read those two rows
> as a bug report, not as protocol character. See
> [docs/fidelity.md](../../fidelity.md).
